TOKEN RADAR

Every protocol that records no token yet, ranked by what its launch would be worth — and how to farm it before it lands.

How the board is built

The radar learns its yardstick from the snapshot itself. For each category it takes the median market-cap / TVL multiple of the protocols whose token already trades (shrunk toward the global median when a category has few traded tokens), then prices every pre-token protocol as implied = TVL × multiple × momentum × maturity × breadth — momentum from its 7-day TVL change, maturity from how long it has run with no token, breadth from its chain count. Protocols already trading are carried at their recorded market cap and flagged, never mixed into the pre-token claim.
