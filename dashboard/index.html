TOKEN RADAR

Every launch candidate that records no token yet, ranked by what its launch would be worth — and how to farm it before it lands.

How the board is built

The radar learns its yardstick from the snapshot itself. For each category it takes the median market-cap / TVL multiple of the protocols whose token already trades (shrunk toward the global median when a category has few traded tokens), then prices every pre-token protocol as implied = TVL × multiple × momentum × maturity × breadth — momentum from its 7-day TVL change, maturity from how long it has run with no token, breadth from its chain count. A protocol counts as already trading when the snapshot records a token for it — a CoinGecko id or a symbol, the snapshot's own test. Trading protocols are carried at their recorded market cap; where the snapshot reports no cap for a token it records, the protocol is carried at TVL × its category multiple — the same yardstick, with no launch adjustments — and flagged, never mixed into the pre-token claim.

Before any of that, a launch-eligibility screen — the same rule the snapshot itself uses to build its watchlist — removes the entries that record no token yet are not launch candidates at all, because they are wrapped, staked or bridged products of another protocol: the CEX, Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking and Restaked BTC categories (an exchange's balance tracker, a bridge's custodied assets, a staking product's claim on an asset that already trades — the snapshot's own example is WBTC, Bitcoin on Ethereum), products whose name begins with an exchange's name, names carrying the words wrapped, staked or staking, and later-version deployments of protocols whose token already trades. They are excluded from the board, never ranked.
